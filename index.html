Marshall Washington

Graduate Student at the University of Colorado Boulder

Hi! I'm Marshall, a master's student at the University of Colorado Boulder advised by Dr. Alexis Palmer. My research interests primarily lie in Corpus and Computational Linguistics, with an emphasis on multilingual coreference resolution and the evaluation of large language models on discourse related tasks. I'm also deeply inspired by linguistics/CompLing/NLP in my artistic practice, and you can find some of my artistic research projects in my portfolio!

Currently, my thesis work centers on Robust Pronoun Fidelity for Large Language Models in Vietnamese. I've also had the opportunity to work on projects that analyze the application of LLMs for the production of glossolalia, and we will be presenting our work at NLP4DH 2026 !

Outside of research, you can find me baking bread or watching reality TV :p
